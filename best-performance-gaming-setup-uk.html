What matters most for performance?

Build order

01

Mouse shape and clear movement space

A shape that fits your grip plus enough room to move is more useful than chasing maximum DPI.

02

Keyboard layout and actuation

TKL preserves essential keys while creating extra mouse room. Adjustable actuation is valuable only if you will configure it.

03

Stable audio and comfort

Comfort, wireless reliability and platform support matter more over long sessions than exaggerated audio claims.

04

Repeatable seating and screen position

A properly compatible monitor arm can make it easier to maintain a consistent viewing position.

Trade-offs in this build

Know before buying

  • Right-handed mouse: the DeathAdder shape is not appropriate for left-handed use.
  • Charging: wireless mouse and headset convenience adds battery management.
  • Configuration: premium keyboard features provide value only when adjusted to your preferences.
  • Desk compatibility: the monitor arm needs a suitable VESA display, desk edge and weight rating.

Compatibility checklist

Before ordering

  • Confirm the keyboard generation, layout and switch technology.
  • Check your hand preference, grip and available mouse space.
  • Confirm headset compatibility with every platform you intend to use.
  • Check the monitor's VESA pattern and weight against the arm specifications.
  • Measure desk depth and edge clearance before buying.

Performance setup FAQs

Common questions

Will this setup improve frame rate?

No. These are peripherals and desk upgrades. Game performance and frame rate mainly depend on the PC, display, settings and software.

Do I need adjustable actuation?

No. It is a preference feature. A reliable conventional keyboard can perform well if its layout and switches suit you.

Is wireless suitable for gaming?

Gaming-focused wireless products can be practical, but confirm connection type, receiver requirements, charging and platform support.

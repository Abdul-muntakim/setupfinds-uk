The best PS5 accessories solve a problem you already have.

This guide is research-led. We compare specifications, compatibility and recurring owner feedback; we do not claim hands-on testing where none occurred.

Quick answer

Choose for your real use case first, then compare features. Avoid paying for a specification you will not notice.

01

Storage before cosmetics

An internal M.2 SSD is useful for large libraries. It must meet Sony’s requirements and needs appropriate heat dissipation.

02

Charging reduces clutter

A dock is worthwhile for two controllers; one controller may only need a good cable.

03

Audio depends on where you play

A headset helps late-night play. Confirm PS5 wireless compatibility before buying.

No-cost improvements

Optimise the setup you already own

  • Centre the display: keep the main screen directly in front of your usual seated position.
  • Make mouse room: move chargers and accessories away from your dominant-hand side.
  • Control cables: route power separately from frequently moved charging cables.
  • Protect comfort: place controls where shoulders stay relaxed and wrists are not forced upward.
  • Change one thing at a time: it becomes easier to tell whether an upgrade genuinely helped.

Common questions

PC gaming setup FAQs

What should I buy first for a gaming setup?

Start with the item causing the biggest comfort or control problem. For many players that is the mouse, headset or monitor position—not decoration.

Is wireless gear good enough for gaming?

Modern gaming-focused wireless products can be a practical choice. Check connection type, charging or battery requirements and platform compatibility before buying.

Do I need an expensive mechanical keyboard?

No. Layout, switch feel and reliability matter more than price alone. A modest keyboard that suits your preferences can be the better purchase.
